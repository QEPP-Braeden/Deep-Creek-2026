State Park · Garrett County, MD

Deep Creek Lake State Park

Maryland's largest inland body of water, surrounded by forested hills. The state park offers shoreline access, quiet hiking trails, and some of the best sunset views in the region.

State Park · Oakland, MD

Swallow Falls State Park

A short, beautiful loop trail through Maryland's largest stand of old-growth hemlocks leads past four waterfalls — with Muddy Creek Falls as the 53-foot showstopper. Rarely crowded, never disappoints.

Park Info

Resort · McHenry, MD · Optional

Wisp Resort — Mountain Coaster

Maryland's only ski resort doubles as a year-round outdoor playground in spring. The gravity-powered mountain coaster gives you sweeping views over the Allegheny highlands — quick, fun, and no ski experience needed.

Wisp Resort Site

Stargazing Option #2

Drive 5–10 minutes away from the lake lights, find an open field or pulloff, and bring blankets. Use a stargazing app like Stellarium or Sky Map. The Milky Way is sometimes visible from this area on clear nights.
